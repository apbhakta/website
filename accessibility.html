Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Velkommen Inn is committed to making both our hotel and our website accessible to all guests, including guests with disabilities.

Our website

We work to conform this website to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. Accessibility features of this site include: full keyboard navigation with visible focus indicators and a skip-to-content link; text alternatives for meaningful images; sufficient color contrast; properly labeled forms; screen-reader landmarks and headings; support for browser zoom and text resizing; and respect for reduced-motion preferences. Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and we review the site as content changes.

Our hotel

Velkommen Inn offers ADA-accessible guest rooms in both King and Double Queen configurations, featuring accessible bathrooms and wider doorways. Accessible parking is available on-site, our seasonal outdoor pool is equipped with an ADA pool lift, and our indoor corridors provide weather-protected access from parking to guest rooms. Service animals as defined by the ADA are welcome at no charge throughout the property.

Requesting an accommodation

If you need an accessible room, have questions about specific accessibility features, or require any accommodation before or during your stay, please contact us and we will gladly assist: Phone: (254) 675-8999 (24-hour front desk) · Email: info@velkommeninncliftontx.com. We recommend reserving accessible rooms by phone so we can confirm availability and features directly.

Feedback

If you experience any difficulty accessing content on this website, or notice something we can improve, please tell us. Contact us at (254) 675-8999 or info@velkommeninncliftontx.com with a description of the issue and the page where it occurred, and we will work to address it promptly.

We value your privacy

We use essential cookies to make this site work, and optional analytics to understand how guests use it. Embedded Google Maps may set their own cookies when viewed. See our Privacy Policy.
