Amenities

Everything you need, done well.

No resort fees, no surprises — just the amenities that actually make a stay better.

Complimentary Breakfast

Continental breakfast served daily, 6:00–9:00 AM. A simple, convenient start before work, travel, or a day exploring Bosque County.

Free Parking & EV Charging EV: fee applies

Free on-site parking near the entrance, with room for work trucks. Level 2 EV charging is available for an additional fee.

Free High-Speed Wi-Fi

Complimentary wireless throughout the property — stream, work, or video-call without a second thought.

Seasonal Outdoor Pool

Cool off and unwind — a great spot for families after a day at the lake or on the road.

Fitness Center

Keep your routine on the road with our on-site fitness center, available during your stay.

Business Center

Print boarding passes, copy documents, or handle quick work needs without leaving the hotel.

Guest Laundry

On-site guest laundry — a lifesaver on extended stays, work assignments, and family trips.

Indoor Corridors

Enter through the lobby and reach your room fully out of the weather — easier with luggage, gear, and kids.

Accessible Rooms

Dog-Friendly Rooms Fee applies

Dogs welcome in designated rooms with advance approval — max 2 dogs. See our pet policy.
