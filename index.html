About

Velkommen Inn

"Velkommen" means welcome in Norwegian — and in Clifton, the Norwegian Capital of Texas, that's more than a name. Our independently owned hotel sits on Highway 6 at 1215 N Avenue G, in the building longtime visitors will remember as the Best Western. Today it carries a name rooted in this community's heritage, with the same convenient location and a renewed commitment to genuine small-town hospitality.

Whether you're here for a weekend at Lake Whitney, a work assignment in Bosque County, a family gathering, or just a comfortable night on the road, you'll find clean rooms, a free continental breakfast each morning, indoor corridors out of the weather, and a front desk that answers 24 hours a day.

We can't wait to host you. Velkommen!
