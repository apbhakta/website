Welcome to Clifton

One of Central Texas' most distinctive small towns.

Clifton combines Norwegian heritage, a thriving arts community, historic downtown charm, outdoor recreation and beautiful Bosque County scenery — earning its place among the most memorable things to do in Clifton TX and the surrounding countryside.

Whether you're visiting for a weekend getaway, wedding, family event, business trip, sporting event or simply exploring Central Texas, Velkommen Inn makes a convenient home base for your Clifton adventure.

Why Clifton Is Special

Three reasons this town stays with you.

Norwegian Heritage

Clifton's Norwegian heritage dates back to settlers who made this part of Texas home in the 1800s. Today, visitors can explore historic churches, pioneer sites and traditions that remain an important part of Clifton's identity.

Art & Culture

From the Bosque Arts Center and local galleries to downtown murals and cultural events, Clifton has developed one of Central Texas' most distinctive small-town arts communities.

Small-Town Texas Charm

Browse locally owned shops, enjoy local restaurants, explore historic streets and experience the friendly atmosphere of the Bosque River Valley.

A Perfect Day in Clifton

Sunrise to stars, without leaving Bosque County.

Morning

Start with breakfast at Velkommen Inn, then head into historic downtown Clifton for coffee, shopping and photos around downtown.

Midday

Visit the Bosque Museum and discover Clifton's Norwegian and pioneer history.

Afternoon

Explore the Bosque Arts Center, a local gallery, or spend some time outdoors along the Bosque River.

Evening

Enjoy dinner at a local Clifton restaurant, catch a movie at the historic Cliftex Theatre, then return to Velkommen Inn for a comfortable night's stay.
